gmail-import

gmail-import is a personal, non-commercial command-line tool. It is used only by its owner to search their own Gmail mailbox and save selected messages and attachments to their own computer.

  • Access is read-only (gmail.readonly scope). The tool never sends, modifies or deletes email.
  • It runs locally on the owner's computer. There is no server, no website login and no public sign-up.
  • It is not offered to the general public.
